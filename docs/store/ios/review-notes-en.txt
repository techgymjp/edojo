1. Purpose and audience: Edo Castle: Moat Ninja Patrol is a free 3D action game for ages 13+. The player steers a small boat around the moats of Edo Castle and catches 88 ninja with thrown coins and a lasso before they climb the stone walls into the castle keep.

2. How to use: No login or account is needed. Tap START on the title screen. Steer with the Fwd / Back / L / R buttons; use Coin, Lasso and Jump. The game is shown in English or Japanese depending on the device language. The app is designed for iPhone in landscape and runs on iPad in iPhone compatibility mode.

3. External services: Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads, without mediation) with Google's consent form (UMP) and App Tracking Transparency. Rewarded ads are optional: when a ninja gets into the keep, watching one lets the player defeat the intruder and keep playing (up to 5 times per game). No other services or in-app purchases.

4. Regional differences: None, except that the ad consent form appears only where required (e.g. EEA/UK). The app shows the same features during and after review.

5. Third-party material: Terrain and water are processed from GSI (Geospatial Information Authority of Japan) tiles, and buildings from Project PLATEAU (MLIT, CC BY 4.0) open data; the sources are shown on the title screen. All other graphics, music and sounds are our own.

6. Guideline 4.3 (Design - Spam): This is a single, original app, not a template, reskin or repackaged version of another app.
- Unique concept and gameplay: a boat-chase game in the moats of Edo Castle. Ninja glide from rooftops into the water, swim underwater breathing through bamboo tubes (shown as glowing tubes) and climb the castle walls; the player catches them with coins and a lasso, jumps over earthen bridges and uses warps between unconnected moats. 88 ninja are released over 10 minutes, and one ninja reaching the keep ends the game.
- Unique content: the Edo-period moats of Edo Castle, including moats and ponds that no longer exist (such as Tameike), restored on top of modern Tokyo; hand-made landmarks (castle keep, turrets, stone walls, more than 30 castle gates, Hie Shrine, the State Guest House, bridges, railways); its own boat, constable and ninja characters and animations; original period-drama music; its own icon, screenshots and metadata.
- What is shared: TECH GYM K.K. is a small developer and reuses only its own technical foundation across its games (the Capacitor app shell, AdMob and consent code, build scripts, and a 3D city engine based on public open data). No purchased template is used. Our other App Store apps are different games in different genres (JET STRIKE: jet fighter combat; Shutoko Grand Prix: car racing). There is only one version of this app.
- If any specific part of the binary or metadata is considered too similar to another app, please let us know and we will change it.
